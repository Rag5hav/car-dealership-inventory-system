# Prompts Log - Car Dealership Inventory System

This document logs the AI prompts and scaffolding assistance requested during the development of the Car Dealership Inventory System.

> **Development Philosophy & AI Collaboration**:
> AI assistance (Gemini 3.6 Flash / ChatGPT) was leveraged exclusively for **rapid boilerplate generation**, configuration declarations (`package.json`, `tsconfig.json`, `jest.config.ts`), TypeScript DTO interfaces, and initial test suite templates—tasks where manual typing is inefficient.
> All **core business domain logic**, TDD execution, architectural decisions, MongoDB Atlas fallback mechanisms, security guards (`protect`, `adminOnly`, `ADMIN_SECRET_KEY`), and zero-stock validation rules were engineered and implemented manually by **Raghav Juneja (`Rag5hav`)**.
